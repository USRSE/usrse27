## Travel Options


- Detroit Metropolitan International Airport (DTW)
  - The airport is approximately 25 miles from the University of Michigan campus, which is generally a 45-minute drive under standard driving conditions. Current travel guidelines suggest arriving at least 2 hours before your flight! Major hubs are Delta, Spirit, and US Air.
  - The [Michigan Flyer](https://www.michiganflyer.com/) bus is one way to get to and from DTW. Prepaid fare is $16 one way/$27 round trip. Tickets purchased at the coach are $3-$6 higher.
  - The [Ann Arbor Visitor's Bureau](https://www.annarbor.org/plan/getting-here-getting-around/) maintains a listing of additional airport shuttle options.
  - Uber and Lyft are available at DTW, and the ride to Ann Arbor is generally $40-$60 depending on traffic and time of day.
  
- Amtrak Train Service
  - The Amtrak station in Ann Arbor is located at 325 Depot Street, Ann Arbor, MI 48104. The station is approximately 1 mile from the Michigan Union. Taxi or rideshare are available for transportation to the campus. More information can be found on the [Amtrak site](https://www.amtrak.com/stations/arb).
    
The  [Ann Arbor Visitor's Bureau](https://www.annarbor.org/meetings/transportation/) provides additional information on travel options to Ann Arbor, including flights, trains, and buses.

## Parking

There is metered parking available on the streets surrounding the Michigan Union; however, it is limited and fills up quickly during the school year. There is also a visitor lot at 400 Thompson Street (about a block away from the Union), as well as a public parking structure at 324 Maynard Street, about 3 blocks from the Union. Additional parking information can be found on the Ann Arbor Downtown Development Authority's [Parking Page](https://www.a2dda.org/park/).

## Public transportation

The Ann Arbor Area Transportation Authority (AAATA) provides bus service throughout the city. More information can be found on the [AAATA site](https://www.theride.org/). 

In addition, the University of Michigan provides a free bus service available to the public. If you see a big blue bus with a yellow "M" on the side, you can hop on and ride for free! More information can be found on the [UM Transportation Services site](https://ltp.umich.edu/campus-transit/routes-and-schedules/).
