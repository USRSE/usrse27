USRSE'27 is built by the community that attends it. Submissions and the call for reviewers open in
mid-February 2027. Detailed guidelines for each track will be published alongside the call.

## Submission tracks

We expect to accept submissions in the following tracks:

- **Papers** &mdash; full-length work on research software practice, tooling, or the people and
  processes behind it.
- **Talks** &mdash; a presentation of your work, your team, or a lesson learned.
- **Tutorials** &mdash; hands-on sessions teaching a tool, technique, or practice.
- **Workshops** &mdash; half- or full-day sessions on a focused theme.
- **Birds of a Feather (BoFs)** &mdash; informal, discussion-driven gatherings around a shared interest.
- **Notebooks** &mdash; executable, reproducible submissions demonstrating research software in use.
- **Posters** &mdash; visual presentations, with abstracts due later than the other tracks.
- **Microtalks** &mdash; short, high-energy presentations.

Bring your story of impact. The USRSE'27 theme is *From Code to Impact: translating research
software into real-world outcomes*, and we are especially interested in submissions that show
research software making a difference beyond the code itself.

## Key dates

Submissions open in mid-February 2027, and most tracks are due in mid-April 2027. Poster abstracts
are due in late June 2027. See the [important dates]({{ site.baseurl }}/about/dates/) page for the
full schedule. Submission deadlines are tentative and subject to change.

## Review for us

We will issue a call for reviewers alongside the call for submissions in mid-February 2027.
Reviewing is one of the most direct ways to shape the program, and we welcome reviewers at every
career stage.

## Other ways to take part

- [Join a planning committee]({{ site.baseurl }}/about/) &mdash; we are recruiting
  committee chairs now.
- [Sponsor the conference]({{ site.baseurl }}/sponsor/) &mdash; sponsorship supports student travel
