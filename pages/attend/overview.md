## Venue Information

[University of Michigan Union](https://uunions.umich.edu/union/)

October 25-27, 2027

USRSE'27 will be hosted
by the University of Michigan with support from LSA Technology Services.

Sessions, tutorials, posters and activities are all under one roof on Central Campus, and the Michigan
Union is walkable to hotels, restaurants, shopping and museums.

- ADA Accessibility

  - If special accommodations are required, please contact the organizers at [usrse27-conference@us-rse.org](mailto:usrse27-conference@us-rse.org)
- Gender Inclusive Resources
  - [Gender-neutral facilities](https://www.google.com/maps/d/viewer?mid=1umHhojMj-3dTngpk40-o8F_ipbI&hl=en_US&ll=42.281551783001895%2C-83.70626125&z=13) on all floors of the Michigan Union
  - [Lactation room](https://hr.umich.edu/benefits-wellness/work-life/lactation-resources/ann-arbor-lactation-rooms-across-campus-michigan-medicine): Room 2042 on the 2nd floor of the Michigan Union
